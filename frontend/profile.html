How Requirements Are Determined

AnumatiSetu evaluates your business characteristics against statutory Indian regulations:

  • Factories Act 1948: Triggered for manufacturing units with 10+ workers.
  • SPCB Air/Water Acts: Applicable for manufacturing, chemical, food & textile units.
  • EPFO / ESIC Acts: Triggered by employee headcounts (≥10 and ≥20).
  • Fire Safety NOC: Mandatory building & industrial safety clearance.
  • Specialized Acts: FSSAI for food units, PESO for hazardous chemicals, IBR for boilers.
